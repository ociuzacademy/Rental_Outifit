Our Story
From a Wardrobe
to a Movement

It started in a small Kochi apartment where our founder, Meera Nair, stood before a closet full of expensive sarees and lehengas worn only once. She asked herself — why do we own what we barely use?

In 2019, DRAPE launched as Kerala's first premium outfit rental platform. Today we serve thousands of customers across South India, partnering with over 80 Indian designers to make luxury fashion accessible, circular, and joyful.

Every outfit in our collection is inspected, professionally cleaned, and curated with love — so that when it arrives at your door, it feels like it was made for you.

Planet & Purpose
Fashion That
Gives Back

The fashion industry is one of the world's largest polluters. At DRAPE, we believe renting is one of the most powerful acts of conscious consumption you can make.

Every outfit rented instead of bought saves water, reduces textile waste, and extends a garment's life cycle — without compromising on beauty or luxury.

01
Eco Dry Cleaning All garments cleaned using GOTS-certified, waterless methods that protect fabric and planet.
02
Zero Single-Use Packaging Every outfit ships in reusable garment bags. Our boxes are 100% recycled and recyclable.
03
Circular Wardrobe Programme Donate your pre-loved designer outfits to DRAPE's resale archive and earn rental credits.
